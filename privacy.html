Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Applies to the Loopback Android app by Orvo. Loopback is for players aged 13 and over.

Who we are

Loopback is published by Orvo. Questions about this policy go to sherifbeshr97@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your progress lives on your device: your scores, levels, stars, Shards, settings and cosmetics. The game has no account and no sign-in, and we run no server that stores your play. Android's own backup may copy the save file to your Google account, under Google's rules and your device settings.

What third parties handle

  • Google Play Billing, through RevenueCat (purchases). When you buy Gems or Loopback+, Google Play processes the payment. RevenueCat, which we use to check purchases on your phone, receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Google AdMob (ads). The game shows optional rewarded ads, which you can watch to continue a run. AdMob uses your advertising ID to deliver ads. In the UK and the European Economic Area, AdMob shows a consent form first, and you choose whether ads are personalised. You can change this any time in your device's ads settings or in the consent form.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (planned). If we turn these on, we will update this page first. They would send anonymous usage events and crash reports, with no name or email.
  • Google Nearby Connections (planned, for local two-player games). It finds phones nearby over Wi-Fi, hotspot or Bluetooth. Nothing from it is sent to us, and the connection is between the phones.

Each of these companies handles data under its own privacy policy: Google and RevenueCat.

What we do not do

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location. We do not keep an account for you, we do not sell your data, and we do not read your files, contacts or messages.

How we use information

The data handled by the companies above is used to run purchases, show ads you agree to, and (once enabled) fix crashes and see which parts of the game people enjoy. We do not combine it with other data about you.

Your choices and rights

You can clear the game's data at any time with Android's Clear data option for the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything on your phone. You can turn off personalised ads in your device's ads settings, or change your consent in the game's consent form.

To ask for a copy or deletion of data held by RevenueCat or by us, email us. Purchase and ad records are also held by Google, and you can ask Google directly. If you are in the UK or EEA, you can see, correct, delete or restrict data held about you, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Data sent to the companies above travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Children

Loopback is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child under 13 has sent us information, email us and we will remove it.

Changes

We will update this page if the app's data handling changes, for example when analytics or local multiplayer ship. The date at the top shows the latest version.
